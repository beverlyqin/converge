## Delta SkyMiles Reserve Card Members

U.S. Basic and Additional Delta SkyMiles® Reserve and Reserve Business American Express Card Members receive complimentary access to The Centurion® Lounge when flying on a same-day Delta-marketed or Delta-operated flight. The eligible flight must be booked on a U.S. issued American Express Card. A Card Member may bring up to two guests at a per-visit rate of $50 USD (or $30 USD for children aged 2 through 17) per person, per location. If you choose to visit multiple locations of The Centurion Lounge and/or Sidecar by The Centurion® Lounge in the same day, guest fees will apply at each location. Guests must be traveling on a same-day Delta-marketed or Delta-operated flight. All guests must be traveling on the same flight as the Card Member. Eligible lounges include all Centurion Lounges located in the United States and select international locations (as set forth on the Centurion Lounge Website). Guest access policies (including but not limited to guest fees and number of complimentary guests per visit) applicable to international locations of The Centurion Lounge may vary by location and are subject to change. Please visit thecenturionlounge.com to learn more about guest access policies for international locations of The Centurion Lounge. To access The Centurion Lounge, the Card Member must arrive within 3 hours of their departing flight, and present The Centurion Lounge agent with the following upon each visit: their valid Reserve or Reserve Business Card and boarding pass showing a confirmed reservation with a seat/boarding zone assignment for an eligible Delta Air Lines flight. Name on boarding pass must match name on the Card. Card Members may be asked to present the Centurion Lounge agent with a government-issued I.D. as a part of the check-in process. Failure to present these items may result in access being denied. During a layover, Card Members must arrive within 5 hours of the connecting flight. Same-day round trip itineraries are not considered connecting flights. Card Members will not be compensated for changes in locations, rates or policies. A Card Member must be at least 18 years of age to enter without a parent or legal guardian. For locations with a self-service bar, the Card Member must be of legal drinking age in the location's jurisdiction to enter without a parent or legal guardian. Must be of legal drinking age to consume alcoholic beverages. Please drink responsibly. American Express reserves the right to remove any person from The Centurion Lounge for inappropriate behavior or failure to adhere to rules, including, but not limited to, conduct that is disruptive, abusive or violent. Access is subject to space availability. Hours may vary by location and are subject to change. Amenities vary among The Centurion Lounge locations and are subject to change. Services and amenities in The Centurion Lounge are complimentary, however, you are responsible for any purchases and/or servicing charges you authorize our Member Services Professionals to perform on your behalf. American Express will not be liable for any articles lost or stolen or damages suffered by the purchaser or visitor inside The Centurion Lounge. Use of The Centurion Lounge is subject to all rules and conditions set by American Express. American Express reserves the right to revise the rules at any time without notice.
