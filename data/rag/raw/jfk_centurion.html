Find & Visit

Terminal
4
Level 4

The Lounge is in Terminal 4. After you go through security, you can find it just beyond the TSA checkpoints to the left of the escalators that take passengers downstairs to the gate level. If you are arriving on the gate level from a connecting flight, please head towards the A concourse and take the stairs or elevator up to the 4th floor to find the lounge.

Hours: 5 am - 10 pm

Learn more about who can access the lounge.

Enjoy

Step into a Lounge so spacious it boasts two premium bars—and a speakeasy. Relax with a cocktail and enjoy access to our new Digital Library of newspapers and magazines. Or get productive with high-speed wifi and a specialty coffee bar. Either way, you’ll depart refreshed. The Centurion Lounge is proud to serve Irving Farm's fresh roasted, New York Coffee on both drip and espresso.

Eat

Savor an inspired menu designed by award-winning talent during your next trip. The Culinary Collective by The Centurion® Lounge™ provides curated selections from a team of celebrity Chefs at select Centurion Lounge locations. Inspired by Resy’s top hits, carefully selected to elevate your journey.

Drink

Find your perfect meal pairing with a thoughtful selection of signature cocktails and non-alcoholic beverages, alongside wines curated by award-winning Sommelier Helen Johannesen of Helen's and Jon & Vinny's.

L'OCCITANE

Inspired by his ancestors’ traditions, Olivier Baussan founded L’OCCITANE over 30 years ago in the Provence region of France. All L’OCCITANE skincare, body care, fragrance and home products are passionately created to preserve the authentic ingredients and traditions of the Mediterranean land. To give greater accessibility, L’OCCITANE has incorporated Braille labeling on its boxes and containers where possible. L’OCCITANE products are available online and in 170 boutiques throughout the U.S.

Sonic Editions

Sonic Editions specializes in limited-edition fine-art photographic prints of the greatest musicians, film stars, cult heroes, and cultural icons. With an unrivaled number of specially sourced iconic pictures from the world's best photographers and most renowned archives, its offering includes photographs rarely seen before. All its prints are expertly mounted and encased in handmade wooden frames. Framed prints start from just $129 making great fine-art photography affordable for the first time. The handpicked collection of photography featured at The Centurion® Lounge is also available for purchase.

12.29, Olfactive Branding

12.29 is an olfactive branding company that creates a scent for your brand, a scent for your home and a scent for your life. In a world where branding is everything, 12.29 takes an innovative approach by creating custom branded scents for retail and corporate environments, hotels, restaurants, nightclubs, fashion runway shows, art fairs, special events, and private homes. Designing scents specifically for a brand identity, 12.29 has collaborated with clients in New York, Paris, Milan, Miami, London, Hong Kong, Australia and China.
